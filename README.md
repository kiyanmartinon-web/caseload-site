# Casebound — public site (case-bound.com)

Every push to `main` is published automatically by Netlify.

- `index.html` — the homepage: what Casebound does, with links into each tool. Text for all 8 languages is in `landing-i18n.js`. The hero shows the real top four results the research tool returns for its example case, and the coverage bar shows document counts per jurisdiction; both are written into the page, so update them when the corpus changes.
- `research.html` — the public research tool (no Intake / corpus admin). Was `index.html` until October 2026; links to `/#cases` are forwarded to `/research.html#cases`.
- `brand.css` — the design layer, loaded last in `<head>` on every page and inside the research tool's two panes. It sets the colour tokens every page uses and the type: Schibsted Grotesk for interface text, Newsreader (`--law`) for the law's own words. Change the look here rather than page by page; the dark palette is in `theme.js`.
- Matching (Finder list and the Atlas map's /25 component) is by **legal concepts**, not shared words: a multilingual (EN/IT/FR/DE) concept tree of ~195 concepts (including a General law & legal method area: sources, precedent, interpretation, retroactivity, legality, capacity, unjust enrichment, negotiorum gestio, abuse of rights, international law; criminal law down to homicide, offences against the person, property offences, sexual and drug offences, attempt, complicity, defences, criminal capacity, sentencing and extradition; and civil law down to performance, set-off, assignment, third-party rights, suretyship, loans, insurance, gifts, possession, easements, adverse possession, good-faith acquisition, liability for things and animals, road accidents, maintenance, matrimonial property, wills and forced heirship) is embedded in `research.html`; each document carries curated `concepts` tags plus concepts detected in its text, and similarity is a rarity-weighted cosine over concept vectors, with broader parent concepts giving partial credit. The corpus (~745 provisions and rulings from Italy, France, Germany, the UK, the US, the EU and international law) and the concept tree are the `CORPUS` and `CONCEPTS` constants inside the Finder's `srcdoc` in `research.html` (the Atlas `srcdoc` holds an identical copy of `CONCEPTS`; keep the two in sync); a document's optional `concepts` array lists concept ids.
- **Case mindmap (Atlas pane)** — maps the case by legal concept: each concept recognised in the case is a *file* on the map that holds the documents sharing that concept (each document filed under its best-fitting concept, with "also in" cross-references). Users can open a file, move documents between files, take them out, add/remove concept files, and download the case file; the arrangement is saved per case in the browser. It reads the document pool and the concept reading of the case from the Finder (`window.CASEBOUND_STATE`), so both panes agree.
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html` — legal pages (all 8 languages).
- `pricing.html`, `login.html`, `account.html` — plans, sign in / create account, and the account + subscription page.
- `risk-check.html` — **Pro feature.** A client describes a problem (type, facts, date, amount, evidence) and gets a Low / Medium / High risk rating with four factors: time limit (simplified Italian limitation periods in the `AREAS` table), evidence, detail and amount. Runs entirely in the browser; nothing is sent. Shown only when `/api/auth/me` returns `tier: "pro"`; everyone else sees what it does and a link to Plans. Starts empty: no example cases. "Find the law behind it" hands the facts to the research tool via `sessionStorage` (`casebound:prefill`). Gating is client-side, like the free-plan limits.
- `can-i.html` — **"Can I…?" (free, daily limit).** Anyone types an everyday legal question; `/api/can-i` (`netlify/functions/canask.mjs`, `netlify/lib/canask.mjs`) asks Anthropic's Claude model and returns a Yes / No / It depends verdict, a per-country breakdown (Italy, Spain, France, Germany, Portugal, Poland, UK, US — the visitor's own country first), conditions, consequences, the rules behind it and next steps, in the visitor's language. Limits are enforced server-side: 5 a day for everyone (free, signed out and Pro) (`LIMITS` in `lib/canask.mjs`; the Plans page shows `canAskPerDay` from `plan-limits.js`). Counts live in Netlify Blobs store `canask` (per account, or a daily-salted IP hash) and are deleted after two days; question text and answers are never stored by Casebound (see *Self-correcting "Can I…?" answers* below for what the learning loop keeps). Recent questions are kept only in the browser (`casebound:caniRecent`). "Find the law behind it" hands the issue to the research tool via `casebound:prefill`.
  - **Netlify env vars:** `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (required; until it is set the page says the feature isn't switched on yet), `CANI_MODEL` (optional, default `claude-sonnet-5-5`; `claude-haiku-5-5` is cheaper), `CANI_DAILY_CAP` (optional, all visitors together per day, default 400 — protects the API bill).
- **Client and lawyer profiles + lawyer connections.** Sign-up asks "client" or "lawyer" (`login.html`); lawyers add bar association, register number, city, areas and bio and start as `pending`. Profile type and lawyer details are edited on `account.html` (changing bar details sends a verified lawyer back to `pending`).
  - **Check and publish in one step.** The risk-check form has a *Publish to lawyers* box (`#rc-publish`, pre-ticked when opened at `#connect`). Ticked, it asks for a one-line summary, other side, region, city, goal and consent, and the button becomes *Check my risk and publish*: the check runs and the case is sent to verified lawyers (`/api/cases/submit`, Pro checked server-side, max 5 open cases) with the **full analysis** attached as `analysis` (risk level, the four factors, evidence breakdown, what's missing, warnings), as plain text in the client's language (`analysisSnapshot` in `risk-check.html`, clipped by `cleanAnalysis` in `lib/matches.mjs`). Not ticked, the result offers *Publish this case*. Cases live in Netlify Blobs store `matches` (`case/<id>`).
  - The lawyer board shows that analysis in the case panel (*Client's risk check — full analysis*, `analysisHTML` in `lawyers.html`), noting the client's language when it differs. Cases sent before October 2026 have no `analysis` and show only the level, as before.
  - `lawyers.html` — the case board for verified lawyers (`/api/cases/board`): client identity hidden; each lawyer rates strength/evidence privately and chooses I want this case / Ask for more / Pass, with an optional message. Empty until a client sends a case.
  - Clients follow replies on `account.html` (`/api/cases/mine`), see interested lawyers' profiles, and choose **Share my contact details** per lawyer (`/api/cases/share`); only then does that lawyer see the client's name and email. Clients can add details or withdraw.
  - **Verifying lawyers:** set Netlify env var `ADMIN_EMAILS` (comma-separated, e.g. `admin@case-bound.com`). Signed in with that email, `account.html` shows **Lawyers to verify** with Verify / Reject (`/api/cases/lawyers`, `/api/cases/verify`). Register that email yourself before anyone else can. Admin accounts always count as Pro (no subscription needed, `tierOf` in `lib/subscription.mjs`) and can read the lawyer board, but only verified lawyers can rate cases or contact clients.
  - **Admin: view pages as client or lawyer.** Signed in as an admin, Settings → *Admin · view pages as* switches between Admin / Client / Lawyer (kept in the browser, `casebound:viewAs`; `window.CBView` in `theme.js`). A strip under the top bar shows the preview and switches back. As lawyer, the board shows the full rating screen, but ratings stay in the browser and nothing reaches clients. As client, the risk check can really send a case (admins may send even with a lawyer profile), and verified lawyers will see it. The server still applies the admin's real permissions.
  - Deleting an account deletes the client's cases and the lawyer's reviews. Back end: `netlify/functions/cases.mjs`, `netlify/lib/matches.mjs`; subscription look-up shared in `netlify/lib/subscription.mjs`.
- `view-switch.js` — the **Client / Lawyer switch**, shown to everyone in the top bar next to the logo, and it sets which menu `shell.js` shows. **Client:** Can I…?, Find a lawyer (Pro, `/risk-check.html#connect`), Risk check (Pro). **Lawyer:** Case building (`research.html`), Cases & clients (Pro + verified lawyers, `/lawyers.html`). Plans and Sign in/Account on both. Pages declare their side with `data-page`: `client` (can-i, risk-check), `lawyer` (research, lawyers) or `info` (homepage, plans, account, sign-in, legal pages). On a client or lawyer page, picking the other side opens that side's first page (`/can-i.html` or `/research.html`); on an info page it swaps the menu in place (event `cb:side`). The choice is kept in local storage (`casebound:view`); lawyer accounts on client pages are still sent to the case board unless they picked Client (`casebound:role` caches the profile type).
- **Lawyers and Pro:** "Cases & clients" needs Pro *and* verification, checked on the server (`lawyerPro` in `netlify/functions/cases.mjs`). For now verified lawyers get Pro free: `FREE_PRO_FOR_VERIFIED_LAWYERS` in `netlify/lib/subscription.mjs` (set it to `false` to make lawyers subscribe; also update `foundP` in `landing-i18n.js`, which tells lawyers it's free for now).
- **Languages** — `i18n.js` (loaded first on every page) offers English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Polski and العربية (right-to-left) in a dropdown in the top bar. It replaces any `.lang` element (or joins `.masthead-in`), remembers the choice in the browser (`casebound:lang`), honours `?lang=xx`, sets `<html lang dir>` and exposes `CB_I18N` (`get`, `set`, `onChange`, `pick`, `locale`). Each page keeps its own strings in a dictionary keyed by language code and re-renders on `CB_I18N.onChange`; the panes inside `research.html` read `parent.CB_I18N`. Concept names on the mindmap and chips come from `concept-labels.js`. The terms and privacy pages hold one full block per language (anchors suffixed `-it`, `-es`, …); the non-English/Italian versions say the English text prevails. To add a language: add it to `LANGS` in `i18n.js` and `netlify/lib/plans.mjs`, then add a dictionary entry on each page, `theme.js`, `view-switch.js` and `concept-labels.js`. Stripe plan text can be translated with product metadata `name_<lang>`, `description_<lang>`, `features_<lang>`.
- `shell.js` — shared top navigation (it follows the Client / Lawyer switch, see `view-switch.js`; Sign in/Account, current page underlined, 8 languages) and a footer that stays at the bottom of the window, on every standalone page (homepage, pricing, can-i, risk-check, login, account, terms, privacy). Load it after `theme.js`. The research app (`research.html`) and the lawyers' board keep their own top bars. Pages that already update `#top-account` keep doing so; elsewhere `shell.js` labels it from `/api/auth/me`.
- `theme.js` — light/dark/auto appearance (saved in the browser) and the Settings menu in the top bar of every page.
- **Self-improving rankings** — `netlify/functions/learn.mjs` (`/api/learn`). The Finder lets users rate results (Relevant / Not relevant) and correct the recognised concepts. Ratings and corrections act immediately in that browser (local storage); with the opt-in "Help improve rankings" box ticked, ratings and opened results are also sent as concept ids + document id and pooled into per-document totals in Netlify Blobs (store `learning`). Every visitor downloads the pooled model, which adds concepts users found a document useful for, demotes ones they rejected, and nudges scores by at most ±18 points. Taught words (vocabulary) never leave the browser. No env vars needed.
- **Self-correcting "Can I…?" answers** — `netlify/lib/canlearn.mjs`, `/api/can-i/rate`, `/api/can-i/fix` (`netlify/functions/canlearn.mjs`). Each answer is filed under an *issue*: the model's own neutral English `research_query`, never the visitor's words. Issues are matched with TF-IDF cosine similarity (light stemming + everyday synonyms, threshold 0.6). Under each answer readers rate Right / Wrong and flag wrong countries (verified lawyers' votes count ×3); verified lawyers and the admin can correct a country's verdict. When a new question matches an issue with a lawyer correction, a disputed country (Bayesian wrong-rate ≥ 60% with ≥ 3 flags) or unstable past verdicts (top verdict < 65% of ≥ 4 answers), the answer is written again with those notes (`ask({notes})` in `lib/canask.mjs`), and on a close match (≥ 0.75) a lawyer's verdict overrides the model and the country shows *Checked by a lawyer*. Ratings carry a signed token (HMAC, 30 days; optional env `LEARN_SECRET`, otherwise derived from `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). Store `canlearn`.
- **Risk check that learns from lawyers** — `netlify/lib/riskmodel.mjs`, `/api/risk-model`. Every strength rating (1–5) a verified lawyer saves on the case board is a training row (the four factor codes + rating, no text). The penalty points are re-fitted by ridge regression shrunk towards the hand-set rules, and switched on only when 5-fold cross-validation beats the rules on both mean error and low/medium/high accuracy (and with ≥ 15 ratings). `risk-check.html` downloads the points and says *Calibrated with N ratings* when they are on. Keep `AREAS` / `RULES` in `riskmodel.mjs` in step with `risk-check.html`. When a case is deleted its rows are re-keyed as anonymous (`risk/anon/`).
- `insights.html` + `/api/insights` — **admin only** (Account → Learning & accuracy): Can I…? right-rate, re-written vs first answers, per-country Bayesian wrong-rates, a 30-day chart, the issues that need a lawyer's correction (with a form to fix them), the risk model's cross-validated accuracy, confusion matrix and learned vs rule points, and the Finder's most useful / most rejected documents.
- `netlify/functions/` — back end: accounts (`/api/auth/register|login|logout|me|delete|update|email|password|logout-all|export`) and Stripe (`/api/plans`, `/api/checkout`, `/api/session`, `/api/portal`).
- Accounts are stored in Netlify Blobs (store `accounts`): passwords hashed with scrypt, sessions in an HttpOnly cookie. Buying a plan requires an account; the Stripe customer is linked to it after checkout.
- **Forgot password (self-service).** `login.html` → *Forgot your password?* emails a 6-digit code (`/api/auth/forgot`); the user enters it with a new password (`/api/auth/reset`), which signs them in and signs out every other device. Codes: hashed, 15 minutes, 5 wrong tries, max 1/minute and 5/hour per email (`netlify/lib/reset.mjs`). The forgot step answers the same whether or not an account exists. Email goes out over SMTP (`netlify/lib/mail.mjs`), so set in Netlify: `SMTP_USER` = `admin@case-bound.com`, `SMTP_PASS` = a Google **App Password** for that account (Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords). Optional: `SMTP_HOST` (default `smtp.gmail.com`), `SMTP_PORT` (465), `MAIL_FROM`. Until they are set, the page says reset by email isn't switched on yet.
- Netlify Blobs stores are created fresh on every request (`store()` in `lib/store.mjs`, `lib/matches.mjs`, `lib/learn.mjs`): each request gets a short-lived token, and reusing a store from an earlier request fails with "Token expired" (this took sign-in down once).

## Subscriptions (Stripe)

Plans, prices, trials and payment methods are all managed in the Stripe dashboard; the site reads them live.

Netlify environment variables:
- `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` (required) — secret or restricted key. Restricted key needs: Products read, Prices read, Checkout Sessions write, Customer portal write, **Subscriptions read** and **Customers write** (without Subscriptions read the account page can only show what was bought at checkout, not renewals or cancellations).
- `STRIPE_PORTAL_LOGIN_URL` (recommended) — Stripe → Settings → Billing → Customer portal → login link.
- `STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX` — set to `true` once Stripe Tax is configured.

Optional product metadata in Stripe: `order`, `highlight=true`, `trial_days`, `hidden=true`, `name_<lang>`, `description_<lang>`, `features_<lang>` (separated by `|`; lang = it, es, fr, de, pt, pl, ar). `order`, `highlight`, `trial_days` and `hidden` can also be set on an individual **price** (it wins over the product), e.g. `highlight=true` on the yearly price only.

Without `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` the Plans page simply says paid plans are not available yet.
- The admin copy (`casebound-admin-LOCAL.html`) and `supabase-signals-setup.sql` are deliberately **not** in this repo.
