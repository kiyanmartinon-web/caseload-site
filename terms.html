2. What Casebound is and isn't

Casebound helps you explore legal sources. It maps case facts to provisions, ranks rulings and other documents by text similarity, and lets you save your analyses in your browser.

  • It is not legal advice. Rankings and similarity scores are produced automatically from text. They do not assess the merits of your case and are no substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer.
  • No professional relationship. Using Casebound or writing to us does not make us your lawyer and creates no duty of confidentiality or care of the kind a lawyer owes a client.
  • Accuracy. We work to keep the corpus accurate, but laws change and summaries simplify. Always check the official text (for example on Normattiva, EUR-Lex or the court's own database) before relying on any source.

The risk check (Pro)

Pro subscribers can use the risk check. It rates a case Low, Medium or High risk using only what you enter: whether the usual time limit for that type of claim appears to have passed, what evidence you list, how much detail you give and the amount at stake. Time limits are simplified, and some events can pause or restart them. The rating does not assess the merits of your case or predict the outcome, it is not legal advice, and it does not replace a lawyer's assessment. The check runs in your browser: what you type is not sent to us unless you choose to send the case to lawyers.

Connecting with a lawyer (Pro)

Pro subscribers can send a case to lawyers registered with Casebound. Lawyers see your case without your name or email. You decide whether to share your contact details with a lawyer who marks interest. Casebound only puts you in touch. It is not a party to any agreement between you and a lawyer, takes no fee or commission from that agreement, and is not responsible for the lawyer's advice or work. No lawyer–client relationship exists until a lawyer confirms it with you directly. You can withdraw a case at any time from your account page. Each client can have up to five open cases at once.
