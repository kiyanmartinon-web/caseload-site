How Casebound handles your data

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Notice under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

The short version

  • Your case stays with you. The facts you type, the files you add and the cases you save are kept in your own browser. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them.
  • Helping us improve is optional and off by default. If you tick “Help improve rankings”, Casebound sends anonymous signals: which kind of action you took and which corpus document you opened. It never sends your words, your files, or anything that identifies you.
  • Like any website, our hosting provider sees your IP address so it can deliver the page to you.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no selling of data.

2. What stays in your browser

Everything you enter into Casebound is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. That includes case facts, claims, files you add and saved cases. Files are read inside your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

Because this information never reaches us, we do not process it as a controller. You can delete it at any time with Clear all in the Cases tab, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

4. The improvement programme (opt-in)

The checkbox “Help improve rankings” is unticked by default. Casebound sends nothing for this purpose unless you tick it.

If you tick it, each signal contains only:

  • the type of action, such as “an analysis was run”, “a result was opened” or “a filter was changed”;
  • the corpus ID of the document involved (for example IT-24), its position in the results, and the filters in use (jurisdiction and document type);
  • the date (day only) and the version of the consent you gave.

It never contains:

  • the facts, claims or any other text you type, or any file you add;
  • your name, email, account or any identifier that links one visit to another;
  • your IP address. The receiving service is configured not to store it with the signal.

Signals are used only to improve how Casebound ranks and suggests sources. They are not used for advertising, profiling or any decision about you.

Withdrawing consent: untick the box at any time. Signals stop immediately. Withdrawing does not affect signals already sent.

5. Cookies and local storage

Casebound sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for two things:

  • to make the tool work, by keeping your saved cases;
  • to remember your choice on the improvement checkbox.

Both are strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for. Under Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code they do not require consent.

6. Who receives data, and international transfers

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts the website and processes connection data on our behalf under its Data Processing Agreement. Transfers to the US rely on Netlify's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, backed by Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • [DATABASE PROVIDER, e.g. Supabase, EU (Frankfurt) region] stores improvement signals on our behalf, inside the European Union, under its Data Processing Agreement.
  • Google and Cloudflare receive connection data when your browser loads fonts or file readers, as described in section 3. Both participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We do not sell or rent data to anyone.

7. No automated decisions about you

Casebound ranks legal sources by text similarity to help you research. It makes no decision about you that has legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. Its results are a research aid, not legal advice.

4. Il programma di miglioramento (facoltativo)

La casella «Aiuta a migliorare i risultati» è deselezionata di default. Casebound non invia nulla per questa finalità finché non la spunti.

Se la spunti, ogni segnale contiene soltanto:

  • il tipo di azione, ad esempio «è stata eseguita un'analisi», «è stato aperto un risultato» o «è stato cambiato un filtro»;
  • l'identificativo nel corpus del documento coinvolto (ad esempio IT-24), la sua posizione tra i risultati e i filtri attivi (giurisdizione e tipo di documento);
  • la data (solo il giorno) e la versione del consenso prestato.

Non contiene mai:

  • i fatti, le domande o qualsiasi altro testo che scrivi, né i file che aggiungi;
  • nome, email, account o qualsiasi identificativo che colleghi una visita all'altra;
  • il tuo indirizzo IP. Il servizio che riceve i segnali è configurato per non memorizzarlo insieme al segnale.

I segnali servono solo a migliorare il modo in cui Casebound ordina e suggerisce le fonti. Non sono usati per pubblicità, profilazione o decisioni che ti riguardano.

Revoca del consenso: togli la spunta in qualsiasi momento. I segnali si interrompono subito. La revoca non pregiudica i segnali già inviati.
