Needs a lawyer’s look

Issues where readers flag a country as wrong (Bayesian estimate of the wrong-rate ≥ 60% with at least 3 flags) or where past verdicts disagree. Saving a correction applies to every future answer to the same question.

Risk check

Each strength rating a verified lawyer gives a published case (1–5) is a training example. The penalty points are re-fitted with a ridge regression that starts from the hand-set rules, and the fit is switched on only when 5-fold cross-validation shows it matches lawyers better than the rules — on both error and the low / medium / high level.
