Free Can I…?

Ask whether you're allowed to do something.

Type a question in plain words. You get a yes or no, where it's allowed and where it isn't, and the conditions that come with it.

Your question is sent to our AI provider to write the answer. Casebound doesn't keep it.
Example

Can I record a phone call without telling the other person?

It depends

  • ItalyYes, if you take part in the call
  • GermanyNo, recording private words without consent is a crime
  • United KingdomYes, for your own records
  • United StatesDepends on the state: some need everyone's consent
